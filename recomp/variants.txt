# page  address  kind  value  id
#   page: a US page (and base), its sites moved to the other versions (recomp/mips2shape.py); <page>:<version>
#   a site of that version's own, at its address, where the US one could not be moved
#   word: the instruction word run instead while the variant is on
#   after / before: a C++ statement run after / before the instruction while it is on
# The ids:
#   3  the fair rules: each change gives at 60 what the PS2 gives at 30 in a second; on only when
#      a map asking for 30 runs at the engine's full rate (config.ini fps60_mode=1, measures:
#      since v123 a match at 60 runs the PS2's own passes and needs none, docs/FPS60.md)
#   4  the fair rules' motion marks: on while a 0x40 actor runs (the players), set by the
#      platform at each actor's call (ps2world.cpp, the actor loop 0x1228C0), with 3
#   5  the M37's pump: on in the native build (at 30 and 60), the platform's M37Fire / M37Pumped
#      (docs/GAME_FIXES.md)
#   6  the upstream bandwidth's choices up to 4096 kbps (ntmenu2, docs/GAME_FIXES.md): on in the native
#      build, with the platform's bridges of the choices' kbps (0x377840, 0x377800) and of the setter

# The same player code sits at other addresses in each map (tools/fps60_sites.py checks them):
#   the stun recovery block S (n001a 0x41A4FC): each frame the stunned player gets
#     (8 + the presses of the last 32 frames) x the stun maximum x C / 1 000 000
#     S+0x68 addiu t7, t7, 1 counts a press of the 32-frame history (the byte 0xB77, from 8)
#     S+0x88 sll t6, t2, 16 then sra 16: the maximum (0xB6A)
#   the spread decay D (n001a 0x41D218): each frame the weapon's two spreads (0xDC, 0xDE) fall
#     by the weapon's rate (its parameter +4) x the stance factor, D+0x5C / D+0x90 mul.s
#   the camera's yaw step Y (n001a 0x3ED8A8) and its settings C (n001a 0x3EF1D0), in the match's
#     camera NewParallelCameraLocal
#   the motion mark checks M (n001a 0x318940: once when a motion's time passes 5 x a3) and M2
#     (0x3189E0: while it is past), 471 and 109 calls in the players' code (moves, weapons)
#   the P2P sender (base 0x172510): an actor that runs on the drawn half of a frame at 30 (it
#     skips the half the skip mask 0x40 marks) and on every frame at 60, sending the player's
#     records every 3 of its runs (0x172578 li t7, 3): 10 a second at 30, 20 at 60
#
# 3  fair spread: D's decrease halved (x 0.5 after each mul.s): the spread falls as fast per second
#    at 60 frames as at 30
# 3  fair stun: a press counts 2 (S+0x68 addiu 2) and the maximum is halved (S+0x88 sll 15):
#    (8 + 2 presses) x max / 2 a frame; with the history's 32 frames lasting half as long at 60,
#    the recovery per second is the PS2's
# 3  fair camera: the camera's speed (its work + 0x70, set each frame by C: ROTATION SPEED x 0.125
#    + 0.5; C+0x48 add.s) halved: the pitch and the turn toward the player's facing move per frame
#    by the stick or the angle x the speed; the yaw (Y) keeps a part of its last step, delta =
#    0.625 x the last + 341.33 x the stick / 128 x the speed: at 60 0.7906 (the square root of
#    0.625, Y+0x28) and 190.63 (Y+0x38) give the same turn per second and the same ramp
# 4  fair motion marks: a player (a 0x40 actor) runs once a frame at 30 with the step 10, the
#    motion's time moved by 5 each half: its k-th frame sees 10 k - 5; at 60 its j-th frame sees
#    5 j. A mark 5 x a3 passes at 30 on the frame ceil((a3 + 1) / 2), at 60 on the frame a3: the
#    automatic weapons fire every 3 frames at 30 (100 ms, a3 4 and 5) and every 4 or 5 at 60
#    (67 and 83 ms). At 60 a3 becomes 2 x floor(a3 / 2) + 2 at the entry of M and M2: every mark
#    of the players' moves and weapons at the time it has at 30
# 5  M37: the fire state's shot request (li t6, 2) goes through M37Fire (0 while the local
#    player's last M37 shot has not finished its pump: the weapon changed during it), the
#    M37's fired state ending its pump (sb t7, 0x4A(s1): state 1) calls M37Pumped
# 3  fair P2P: at 60 the sender waits 6 of its runs (0x240F0006): 10 sends a second as a PS2 in a
